# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small product teams, agencies, and internal operators who manage shared work in a live kanban workspace. They use the product during active planning, triage, handoff, and review, where clarity, fast scanning, and trustworthy realtime state matter more than expressive decoration.

## Product Purpose

realtime-kanban helps teams coordinate board work with visible collaboration state: presence, edits, locks, conflicts, movement history, and sync feedback. Success means users can understand what changed, who is acting, and what needs attention without leaving the board.

## Brand Personality

Clear, focused, precise. The interface should feel like modern productivity software: calm enough for repeated daily use, structured enough for complex team work, and polished enough to be trusted.

## Anti-references

Avoid template-generated dashboards, playful toy kanban boards, dense enterprise admin screens, decorative glassmorphism, excessive poster typography inside working screens, and generic SaaS visual tropes that make hierarchy harder to scan.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize the work surface: board content, status, and collaboration signals should always beat decoration.
- Make realtime state legible: live, locked, editing, conflict, and offline states need consistent visual weight and clear recovery cues.
- Use earned familiarity: interactions should feel comparable to Linear, Notion, Raycast, Vercel, and modern productivity tools.
- Keep hierarchy quiet but exact: typography, spacing, and contrast should help users scan without shouting.
- Preserve a restrained identity: cobalt and acid accents are product signals, not background decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states. Keyboard focus must be visible, touch targets should meet a 44px minimum where practical, and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Color cannot be the only indicator for collaboration or error states.
